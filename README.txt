SHYAMORA WEBSITE - UPDATED PACKAGE

FILES
- index.html: homepage structure
- style.css: visual design and responsive layout
- script.js: gallery, WhatsApp ordering, and demo notification behavior
- images/shyamora-1.png and images/shyamora-2.png: product photos currently included
- shyamora-hero-video.mp4: hero video

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
3. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the full folder contents to your hosting.

ADD YOUR ORIGINAL PHOTOS
- Replace images/shyamora-1.png and images/shyamora-2.png with your own product images (keep the same filenames), or update the paths in script.js and index.html.
- Create images/ingredients/ and add ingredient-01.jpg through ingredient-08.jpg.
- In index.html, change each “Ingredient name” to the exact ingredient name printed on your actual product label.
- The ingredients gallery uses placeholders until you add those photos. Do not publish ingredient claims that are not confirmed on your label.

DEMO PURCHASE POPUP
- The popup is explicitly marked DEMO and “NOT A REAL ORDER.” It first appears after 12 seconds and then every 4 minutes, for 9 seconds.
- It rotates through 30 sample names. Do not present this as genuine customer activity.
- Before going live, replace the demo behavior with a verified order feed or remove the demo popup.

ORDERING
- WhatsApp ordering is configured for the phone number already present in the supplied website. Verify it is your correct business number before publishing.
- Confirm product prices, shipping, COD and returns before launch.
